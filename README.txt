-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Controls:
How to open:
Double click the 'Catify.exe' file

Download Page:
To download a song put a link to a youtube video into the textbox and click download.

Song List Page:
To select/play a song click the box to the right of the song length.
To delete songs click the same box but while holding either shift key.

Playlists Page:
To create a playlist type the name you want in the textbox and click 'Create Playlist'.
To modify playlists click 'Edit Playlist' in the top right then go to the songs list and select which songs you'd like in the
playlist.
To play the songs in your playlist have that playlist selected then go to the Song List Page.
To delete playlists make sure it's not selected then hold shift and click it.

Searching:
To search be in the Song List page then type what you want to search and press 'Enter' or 'Return'.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

How to create desktop icon:
Right click "Catify.exe" and select "Create shortcut" then you can drag the shortcut to your desktop
and rename it to whatever you want.
